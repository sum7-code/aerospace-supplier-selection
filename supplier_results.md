# Supplier Selection Results

## Objective

The objective of this analysis is to identify the strongest supplier using a weighted scoring model.

The suppliers were assessed using five criteria:

- Cost
- Quality
- Delivery
- Risk
- Sustainability

## Weighting

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Cost | 20% |
| Quality | 25% |
| Delivery | 20% |
| Risk | 20% |
| Sustainability | 15% |

## Supplier Ranking

| Rank | Supplier | Overall Score |
|---:|---|---:|
| 1 | NovaAero | 91.45 |
| 2 | Apex Aerospace | 89.60 |
| 3 | Orion Aerospace | 89.35 |
| 4 | AeroTech | 83.75 |
| 5 | Vector Systems | 82.50 |
| 6 | SkyParts | 82.45 |
| 7 | Titan Components | 77.40 |
| 8 | Falcon Components | 73.55 |

## Recommendation

Based on the weighted scoring model, **NovaAero** is the recommended supplier.

NovaAero achieved the highest overall score of **91.45**. It performed particularly strongly in quality, delivery, risk and sustainability.

Although NovaAero did not have the highest cost score, its strong performance across the other criteria resulted in the highest overall score.

## Business Insight

This analysis demonstrates how a multi criteria decision making approach can support supplier selection rather than relying on cost alone.

For an aerospace supply chain, supplier selection should consider the trade-off between cost, quality, delivery reliability, operational risk and sustainability.

## Methodology

The overall supplier score was calculated using:

**Supplier Score = (Cost × 20%) + (Quality × 25%) + (Delivery × 20%) + (Risk × 20%) + (Sustainability × 15%)**

A higher score represents stronger overall performance.
